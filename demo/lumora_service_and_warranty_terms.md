# Lumora Home Appliances — Service Plans and Warranty Terms (India)

Applies to all Lumora water purifiers, air purifiers and kitchen appliances sold in India on or after 1 January 2026. Customer care: 1800-419-2020 (toll free, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, all days).

## 1. Standard Warranty by Product

The standard warranty starts on the date of installation, or 15 days after the date of purchase if the product is not installed by then, whichever is earlier.

| Product | Standard warranty | Key part warranty |
|---|---|---|
| Aquila X2 water purifier | 2 years | UV LED module, 3 years |
| Aquila X2 Pro water purifier | 3 years | RO membrane, 3 years; heating element, 2 years |
| Aquila Mini water purifier | 1 year | none |
| Zephyr A400 air purifier | 1 year | motor, 5 years |
| Zephyr A700 air purifier | 2 years | motor, 7 years |
| Brava induction cooktop | 1 year | coil, 3 years |

Consumables are never covered by the standard warranty. Consumables include all water filters and membranes (except where a key part warranty above says otherwise), air purifier HEPA and carbon filters, sanitising sachets and cleaning kits.

## 2. Registration

Products must be registered on the Lumora app within 30 days of installation for the warranty to be valid. If a customer cannot use the app, the dealer can register the product on the customer's behalf by calling the dealer helpline. Products that are not registered receive only the minimum statutory warranty of 6 months.

## 3. What the Warranty Does Not Cover

- Damage caused by incorrect installation by anyone other than a Lumora-authorised technician.
- Damage from power surges where the product was not connected through a surge-protected socket. The Aquila X2 Pro has built-in surge protection, so this exclusion does not apply to it.
- Damage caused by water with total dissolved solids (TDS) above the model's rated limit: 2,000 ppm for the X2, 3,000 ppm for the X2 Pro and 1,000 ppm for the Aquila Mini.
- Cosmetic damage, such as scratches and dents, that does not affect operation.
- Products used for commercial purposes, such as in restaurants or offices with more than 20 people.

## 4. Service Visits and Charges

Service requests are raised through the app or by phone. Lumora aims to send a technician within 48 hours in metro cities and within 72 hours elsewhere. During the warranty period, service visits and labour are free. After the warranty ends, a visit charge of INR 399 applies, plus the cost of parts. The visit charge is waived if the customer buys a CarePlus plan during the same visit.

Repairs that cannot be done at home are taken to the nearest Lumora Service Centre. If a repair takes more than 7 days, the customer is offered a loaner purifier free of charge until the repair is complete.

## 5. CarePlus Plans

CarePlus plans extend the warranty and include some consumables. They can be bought at any time while the standard warranty is active, or within 60 days after it ends (an inspection fee of INR 299 applies in that case).

| Plan | Extra cover | Price for Aquila X2 (INR) | Price for Aquila X2 Pro (INR) | Filters included |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CarePlus 1 | 1 year | 1,999 | 2,799 | one sediment pre-filter |
| CarePlus 3 | 3 years | 4,999 | 6,499 | all filters except the RO membrane |
| CarePlus Total 5 | 5 years | 8,499 | 10,999 | all filters including the RO membrane |

CarePlus Total 5 also includes two free sanitisation visits per year. CarePlus plans are transferable once if the product is sold, for a transfer fee of INR 500.

## 6. Replacement Policy

If the same fault occurs three times within 12 months during the warranty period and cannot be repaired, Lumora replaces the product with a new unit of the same or a higher model. The replacement unit carries the remaining warranty of the original product, or 6 months, whichever is longer.

## 7. Refunds and Cancellations

A product may be returned for a full refund within 10 days of installation if it has a manufacturing defect confirmed by a technician. CarePlus plans can be cancelled within 15 days of purchase for a full refund; after that, a pro-rata refund is given minus a cancellation charge of INR 250, and no refund is given if any free filter has already been supplied.

## 8. Data and Privacy

The Lumora app collects water usage, filter life and error codes to provide service reminders. This data is stored on servers in Mumbai and is deleted 24 months after the product is de-registered. Customers can request a copy of their data or its deletion through the app's Privacy menu; requests are completed within 30 days.
