# Helios Analytics Pvt. Ltd. — Leave and Holiday Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Policy owner: People Operations. Version 2.4.

## 1. Leave Year

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Leave is credited monthly. Employees who join part-way through the year receive leave on a pro-rata basis from the month they join.

## 2. Types of Leave

| Leave type | Days per year | Carry forward | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earned leave | 21 | Up to 30 days in total | Credited at 1.75 days per month |
| Sick leave | 10 | Not carried forward | Medical certificate needed for 3+ consecutive days |
| Casual leave | 6 | Not carried forward | Maximum 2 consecutive days |
| Wellness days | 4 | Not carried forward | Company-wide fixed dates, announced in January |
| Volunteering leave | 2 | Not carried forward | For registered non-profit activities |

Earned leave beyond the 30-day carry-forward cap lapses on 31 December. Employees may encash up to 10 days of earned leave per year at their basic salary rate; requests for encashment are made in November.

## 3. Applying for Leave

Leave is requested in the HR system "Peoplepad". Earned leave of five or more days must be requested at least 14 days in advance. Casual leave should be requested at least one working day in advance, except in emergencies. Sick leave can be applied for after the employee returns, within three working days.

Managers must approve or decline leave requests within three working days. If a manager does not respond, the request is approved automatically.

## 4. Parental Leave

- Birth parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave for the first two children and 12 weeks for the third and subsequent children.
- Non-birth parents, including adoptive parents and partners, receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave, which may be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- Adoptive primary caregivers of a child under 3 months old receive 12 weeks of paid leave.

After returning from parental leave, employees may work a reduced schedule of 30 hours a week for up to 8 weeks at full pay.

## 5. Bereavement and Special Leave

Employees receive 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member and 2 days for other relatives. Marriage leave is 3 days and may be taken once during employment. Employees called for jury service or to vote receive paid time off for the time required.

## 6. Public Holidays

Helios observes 10 public holidays a year: 3 national holidays (Republic Day, Independence Day and Gandhi Jayanti) and 7 festival holidays chosen by each office from a list published by People Operations in December. Employees who are required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off, which must be used within 60 days.

## 7. Sabbatical

Employees with at least 5 years of continuous service may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of 1 to 6 months. One sabbatical may be taken every 5 years. Employees with 7 or more years of service receive the first 4 weeks of a sabbatical at full pay. Health insurance continues during a sabbatical.

## 8. Notice Period and Leave

During the notice period, earned leave may only be taken with the manager's approval. Unused earned leave is paid out in the final settlement, up to a maximum of 30 days. Leave taken in excess of the leave balance is deducted from the final settlement at the employee's daily gross salary.
