# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Scholarships and Financial Aid Guide 2026–27

Issued by the Financial Aid Office, Administrative Block B, Room 214. Office hours are 10:00 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday to Friday. The Financial Aid Officer is Mr. Anirudh Kesavan.

## 1. Overview

Every year the Institute distributes about INR 4.2 crore in scholarships, waivers and emergency grants. Roughly one student in five receives some form of financial support. Aid is awarded on the basis of merit, need, or both, and each scheme has its own deadline. A student may hold at most two scholarships at the same time, but the combined value of all awards may not exceed the student's total tuition and hostel charges for the year.

Applications are made through the "Aid" tab of the student portal. Paper applications are accepted only from students who have no reliable internet access, and must be handed in at Room 214 in person.

## 2. Merit Scholarships

**Founder's Merit Scholarship.** Named after Dr. Hari Prasad Venkatesan. Awarded to the top 25 students of each incoming B.Tech batch, ranked by the entrance examination score. The scholarship covers 75 percent of the tuition fee for all eight semesters, provided the student keeps a CGPA of at least 8.0. If the CGPA falls below 8.0 in any year, the scholarship is suspended for the following year and can be restored once the CGPA is back above 8.0. It is not restored for semesters that have already passed.

**Department Rank Award.** A one-time cash award of INR 20,000 given each year to the student with the highest SGPA in each department and year of study. In the event of a tie, the award is split equally.

**Lakeside Research Fellowship.** For M.Tech students who publish a paper in a recognised journal or conference during their programme. The fellowship pays INR 12,000 per month for up to 10 months and is renewable once. Applications are reviewed by the Research Council, which meets in the first week of February and the first week of September.

## 3. Need-Based Support

**Tuition Waiver Scheme.** Students whose annual family income is below INR 6,00,000 may receive a tuition waiver. The waiver depends on income band:

| Annual family income (INR) | Tuition waiver |
|---|---|
| Below 2,50,000 | 60 percent |
| 2,50,000 to 4,00,000 | 40 percent |
| 4,00,000 to 6,00,000 | 25 percent |

The waiver does not cover hostel, mess or examination fees. Income must be documented with an income certificate issued within the previous 12 months. Waiver applications for the Monsoon Semester must be submitted by 15 June, and for the Spring Semester by 20 November.

**Hostel Subsidy.** Students who hold a tuition waiver of 40 percent or more may also apply for a hostel subsidy of INR 15,000 per semester. The subsidy is credited directly against the hostel fee and cannot be paid out in cash.

**Book Grant.** First-year students on the Tuition Waiver Scheme receive a one-time book grant of INR 6,500 in the form of a library-bookshop voucher. The voucher expires 90 days after it is issued.

## 4. Emergency Assistance

The Student Emergency Fund helps students facing a sudden financial crisis, such as a medical emergency in the family or the loss of a parent's income. Grants are up to INR 50,000 per student per academic year and do not need to be repaid. Requests are reviewed within five working days. In urgent medical cases the Dean of Student Affairs can approve up to INR 15,000 on the same day.

Interest-free short-term loans of up to INR 30,000 are also available. They must be repaid within 12 months, in monthly instalments deducted from any stipend or paid through the portal. A student with an unpaid emergency loan cannot receive a degree certificate until the loan is cleared.

## 5. External Scholarships

The Financial Aid Office helps students apply for government and private scholarships. Students who receive an external scholarship must inform the office within 30 days, because some Institute awards are reduced when an external scholarship is received. In particular, the Founder's Merit Scholarship is reduced by the amount of any external tuition scholarship so that the combined tuition support does not exceed 100 percent.

## 6. Work-Study Programme

The Campus Work-Study Programme offers part-time jobs in the library, laboratories, the IT help desk and the Sports Complex. Students may work up to 12 hours per week during the semester and up to 30 hours per week during vacations. The hourly rate is INR 180 for undergraduates and INR 240 for postgraduates. Work-study earnings are paid monthly into the student's bank account on the 5th of the following month.

To be eligible, a student must have a CGPA of at least 6.0 and no disciplinary record. First-semester students may join only after their mid-semester examinations.

## 7. Repayment, Withdrawal and Appeals

If a student withdraws from the Institute before the end of a semester, any scholarship or waiver amount for that semester must be repaid on a pro-rata basis. Appeals against aid decisions can be made to the Financial Aid Appeals Panel within 15 days of the decision. The panel's decision is final.

Students found to have given false information in an aid application must repay the full amount received and may be referred to the Disciplinary Board.
