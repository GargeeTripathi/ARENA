# Helios Analytics Pvt. Ltd. — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Effective date: 1 March 2026. Policy owner: People Operations. Version 3.2.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how employees of Helios Analytics may work away from the office. It applies to all full-time and fixed-term employees in India. It does not apply to interns, who must work from an office for at least four days a week during their first eight weeks, or to contractors, whose arrangements are set in their individual contracts.

## 2. Work Arrangements

Helios offers three arrangements:

- **Office-first:** at least four days a week in an office.
- **Hybrid:** two "anchor days" in the office each week, chosen by the team. For most teams the anchor days are Tuesday and Thursday. The Data Platform team uses Monday and Wednesday.
- **Remote:** fully remote work, approved by the department head and People Operations. Remote employees must still attend the quarterly in-person planning week, called "Helios Converge", which is held in Pune.

New joiners start on the hybrid arrangement and may apply for remote work only after completing their 6-month probation period with a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher.

## 3. Core Hours and Availability

All employees, regardless of arrangement, must be available during core hours of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST on working days. Outside core hours, employees may set their own schedules as long as they complete a 40-hour week. Employees must respond to messages from their manager within two hours during core hours.

Meetings must not be scheduled before 10:00 AM or after 6:30 PM IST unless at least one participant is in a time zone where no other slot is reasonable.

## 4. Home Office Allowance

Employees on the hybrid or remote arrangement receive a one-time home office setup allowance of INR 25,000, which can be claimed within the first 90 days of moving to that arrangement. Eligible items are a desk, an ergonomic chair, a monitor, a keyboard and mouse, and a headset. Laptops are provided by the company and are not covered by the allowance.

Remote employees also receive a monthly internet and utilities allowance of INR 2,200. Hybrid employees receive INR 1,100 per month. The allowance is paid with salary and is taxable.

Receipts must be uploaded to the expense portal "Ledgerly" within 30 days of purchase. Claims submitted later will be rejected.

## 5. Working From Another Location

Employees may work from a location other than their registered home address for up to 30 days per calendar year without approval, provided the location is within India and has a reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps. Working from outside India for any period requires written approval from the Legal team at least 21 days in advance, because of tax and data protection rules. Working from outside India is limited to 15 working days per calendar year.

## 6. Security Requirements

Employees working away from the office must:

- Connect only through the company VPN ("HeliosGate") when accessing internal systems.
- Never use public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
- Lock their screen whenever they step away; laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Store client data only on company-approved systems. Downloading client data to personal devices is a disciplinary offence.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 4 hours of discovery.

## 7. Changing or Ending an Arrangement

An employee may request a change of arrangement once every six months. The manager must respond within 10 working days. Helios may end a remote or hybrid arrangement with 30 days' written notice if business needs change or if performance falls below "Meets Expectations" for two consecutive review cycles.
