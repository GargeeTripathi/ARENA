# Helios Analytics Pvt. Ltd. — Travel and Expense Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2026. Policy owner: Finance. Version 5.0. This policy replaces version 4.3.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers business travel and work-related expenses for all employees of Helios Analytics in India. It does not cover relocation, which is handled under the Relocation Policy, or the home office allowance, which is covered by the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.

## 2. Approvals

All business travel must be approved in advance in the travel tool "Wayfarer". Domestic trips need approval from the employee's manager. International trips need approval from the manager and the department head, and must be requested at least 21 days before departure. Trips that are booked without approval will not be reimbursed unless the department head approves an exception in writing within 7 days of the trip.

## 3. Air and Rail Travel

Employees may fly economy class on all domestic flights. Premium economy is allowed on international flights longer than 6 hours. Business class is allowed only for the CEO and the CFO, or with written approval from the CEO.

Flights must be booked through Wayfarer at least 14 days in advance when possible. For trips booked less than 7 days before departure, the employee must record a reason. Rail travel in AC 2-tier or AC chair car is allowed for journeys under 8 hours.

## 4. Hotels

Hotels must be booked through Wayfarer. The nightly limits, including taxes, are:

| City category | Examples | Nightly hotel limit (INR) |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru | 7,500 |
| Tier 2 | Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad | 6,000 |
| Tier 3 | All other cities | 4,500 |

During Helios Converge week in Pune, the company books a block of rooms at the partner hotel and employees must stay there. Staying with friends or family instead of a hotel earns a flat "home stay" allowance of INR 1,500 per night.

## 5. Daily Allowance

Employees travelling overnight receive a daily allowance (per diem) to cover meals and incidental expenses. No receipts are needed for the per diem.

- Domestic travel: INR 1,800 per day in Tier 1 cities, INR 1,400 per day elsewhere.
- International travel: USD 75 per day, or USD 95 per day in the United States, the United Kingdom, Singapore and Switzerland.

On the day of departure and the day of return, half the daily allowance is paid. If meals are provided by a client or at a conference, the allowance is reduced by 25 percent for each meal provided.

## 6. Local Transport

Taxis and ride-hailing services are allowed for business trips and for travel to client sites. Employees using their own car are reimbursed INR 12 per kilometre; for two-wheelers the rate is INR 5 per kilometre. Parking and tolls are reimbursed at actual cost with receipts. Travel between home and the employee's regular office is not reimbursable, except for late-night travel after 10:30 PM, which is reimbursed up to INR 800 per trip.

## 7. Client Entertainment

Client meals and entertainment must be pre-approved by the manager when the expected cost is above INR 10,000. The limit is INR 3,000 per attendee, including the Helios employees present. Alcohol is reimbursable only when a client is present and is limited to 20 percent of the bill.

## 8. Claims and Reimbursement

Expense claims are submitted in "Ledgerly", the same expense portal used for the home office allowance. Every expense above INR 500 must have a receipt. Claims must be submitted within 45 days of the end of the trip; claims submitted between 45 and 90 days late need department head approval, and claims older than 90 days are rejected. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary if approved by the 20th of the month.

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than six times a year. The monthly limit on a corporate card is INR 1,50,000. Personal expenses must never be charged to a corporate card; if this happens by mistake, the employee must repay the amount within 15 days.

## 9. Misuse

Submitting false or inflated claims is gross misconduct and may lead to dismissal. Finance audits a random sample of 10 percent of claims every quarter.
