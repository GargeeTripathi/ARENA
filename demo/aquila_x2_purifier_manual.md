# Aquila X2 Smart Water Purifier — User Manual

## 1. Overview

The Aquila X2 is a countertop water purifier made by Lumora Home Appliances. It combines a sediment pre-filter, an activated carbon block, a reverse osmosis (RO) membrane and an ultraviolet (UV) LED chamber. A mineral cartridge adds calcium and magnesium back into the purified water after the RO stage. The X2 stores up to 8.5 litres of purified water in a food-grade tank and can purify about 18 litres per hour when the incoming water pressure is between 0.8 and 3.5 bar.

The purifier is designed for incoming water with total dissolved solids (TDS) of up to 2,000 ppm. For water above 2,000 ppm, Lumora recommends the Aquila X2 Pro, which uses a high-rejection membrane.

## 2. Installation

Install the purifier on a flat, level surface within 1.2 metres of a power socket and a cold-water tap. Do not install the unit in direct sunlight or where the room temperature can drop below 4 °C, because freezing can crack the RO membrane housing.

Connect the inlet hose to the cold-water supply using the supplied three-way diverter valve. Connect the drain hose to a sink drain or an outlet at least 15 centimetres below the base of the unit. Before first use, flush the system by running it for 12 minutes and discarding all the water produced. The first two full tanks after installation should also be discarded.

## 3. Control Panel and Indicators

The front panel has three touch buttons: POWER, DISPENSE and HOT-LOCK. The X2 does not heat water; the HOT-LOCK button is reserved for the X2 Pro and has no function on the X2.

The status ring around the DISPENSE button shows the condition of the purifier:

- Steady blue: normal operation, tank full.
- Pulsing blue: purifying, tank filling.
- Steady amber: a filter is due for replacement within 15 days.
- Flashing amber: a filter has expired; water is still dispensed but quality is not guaranteed.
- Flashing red: error. See Section 6.

## 5. Cleaning and Maintenance

Wipe the exterior with a soft, damp cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners or solvents. Sanitise the storage tank every 90 days using the Aquila sanitising sachet (part AQ-SAN1): drain the tank, add one sachet dissolved in two litres of water, wait 20 minutes, then drain and flush with three full tanks of purified water.

If the purifier will not be used for more than 10 days, switch it off at the mains, close the inlet valve and drain the tank. When restarting, discard the first full tank.

## 6. Troubleshooting and Error Codes

When the status ring flashes red, the small display under the panel shows an error code.

- E1 — Low inlet pressure. Check that the inlet valve is open and that the supply pressure is above 0.8 bar. A booster pump kit (AQ-BP2) is available for low-pressure homes.
- E2 — Tank overflow sensor triggered. Switch off, drain the tank and check that the float switch moves freely.
- E3 — UV module fault. The purifier stops dispensing to prevent unsterilised water being served. Contact service.
- E4 — Leak detected in the base tray. Switch off at the mains immediately and close the inlet valve.
- E5 — Membrane pressure too high, usually caused by a clogged sediment pre-filter. Replace AQ-SF10 and reset its counter.

## 7. Warranty

The Aquila X2 carries a 2-year comprehensive warranty from the date of installation, covering parts and labour, provided the unit is registered on the Lumora app within 30 days of installation. Filters and the mineral cartridge are consumables and are not covered. The UV LED module is covered for 3 years. The warranty is void if non-genuine filters are used or if the unit is serviced by anyone other than a Lumora-authorised technician.

Extended warranty plans are available: "CarePlus 1" adds one year for INR 1,999 and includes one free sediment pre-filter; "CarePlus 3" adds three years for INR 4,999 and includes all filter replacements except the RO membrane.

## 8. Specifications

Rated voltage: 220–240 V AC, 50 Hz. Power consumption: 36 W while purifying, 2 W on standby. Dimensions: 270 mm (W) × 410 mm (D) × 445 mm (H). Net weight: 7.8 kg. Recovery ratio: up to 60 percent (the share of incoming water that becomes purified water).
